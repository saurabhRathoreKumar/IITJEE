### 1.3 Active Recall & Error-Logging Protocol

To lock in 5 revisions and retain 100% of formulas and reaction mechanisms:
1. **The 24-Hour Mistake Diary:** Every incorrect question in mocks and daily drills is logged with:
    - Topic name
    - Mistake type: *(A) Conceptual Void*, *(B) Calculation Glitch*, *(C) Question Misreading*
    - Correct method and 1-line key learning.
2. **Formula Flashcards:** Maintain a 1-page condensed micro-sheet for each chapter (formulas, reaction pathways, special boundary conditions).
3. **The 3-Pass Solving Strategy:** Pass 1: Quick direct questions (< 1 min); Pass 2: Calculation-heavy questions (2–3 mins); Pass 3: Multi-concept advanced problems.
